**Microsoft Clarity** records heatmaps and how visitors use your pages. It is free. Cookies and cross-page tracking wait until the visitor accepts cookies, so the **Cookie Consent** service must be installed.

## Set it up

1. In Clarity, open **Settings > Setup** and copy your **Project ID**. It is the code after `clarity.ms/tag/` in the install snippet.
2. Open **Microsoft Clarity Settings**, enter it and tick **Enabled**.
3. Press **Save Settings**.

It only loads on the live site, and is off by default until your site's design includes it, so ask your developer to confirm.
